# My Favorite Series: Who Are You: School 2015
## 2015 ‧ 1 season
### Who Are You: School 2015 is a 18-episode South Korean teen mystery-romance drama that serves as the sixth installment of KBS’s long-running School franchise. The story explores dark social themes such as school violence, bullying, and institutional apathy, wrapped in a high-stakes twin identity-swap mystery.

# My Favorite Series: Culpa Mia
## 2023 ‧ Romance/Drama ‧ 1h 57m
### My Fault (Spanish: Culpa mía) is a 2023 Spanish romantic drama film directed by Domingo González in his directorial feature length debut and starring Nicole Wallace and Gabriel Guevara. The film is based on the Wattpad story of the same name by Mercedes Ron. There were two sequels to form a trilogy, Your Fault in 2024 and Our Fault in 2025. My Fault premiered on June 8, 2023 on Amazon Prime. It became an instant hit, garnered lots of attention on TikTok, and was the most watched film on the platform of 2023.
